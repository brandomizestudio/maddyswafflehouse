- **Usage:** Primary buttons, prices, accents
- **Effect:** Dimensional gold shimmer

### **Navy to Blue Gradient**

## 🌟 Luxury Design Principles Applied

### **1. Sophistication Through Restraint**
- Limited color palette (3 main colors + neutrals)
- Strategic use of gold (not overwhelming)
- Clean, uncluttered layouts

### **2. Premium Material Feel**
- Gradient overlays suggest depth and quality
- Gold shimmers like real metal
- Navy blues evoke velvet and silk

### **3. Timeless Elegance**
- Classic color combinations
- Avoid trendy neon or pastels
- Professional and enduring

### **4. Visual Hierarchy**
- Gold draws attention to key elements
- Navy grounds and structures
- Cream provides breathing room

## 🎨 Color Usage Guidelines

### **DO:**
✓ Use gradients for dimensional effects
✓ Apply gold to highlight important elements
✓ Layer navy and blue for depth
✓ Use cream/beige for warmth

### **DON'T:**
✗ Use pure black (#000000)

✗ Mix in additional accent colors
✗ Use harsh shadows without gold tint
✗ Apply bright, saturated colors
