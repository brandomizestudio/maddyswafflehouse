# Maddy's Waffle House - Image Guide

## Current Images

Your website now uses **local images** stored in the `images/` folder. This makes it easy to replace them with your own photos!

### Folder Structure
```
images/
├── hero.jpg                    (Hero section background)
├── about.jpg                   (About section image)
├── products/
│   ├── chocolate-waffle.jpg    (Belgian Chocolate Overload)
│   ├── berry-waffle.jpg        (Fresh Berry Bliss)
│   └── nutella-waffle.jpg      (Nutella & Banana Crunch)
└── gallery/
    ├── gallery-1.jpg           (Waffle close-up)
    ├── gallery-2.jpg           (Coffee art)
    ├── gallery-3.jpg           (Cafe seating)
    ├── gallery-4.jpg           (Waffle making)
    ├── gallery-5.jpg           (Cozy corner)
    └── gallery-6.jpg           (Drinks)
```

## How to Replace Images

1. **Take or prepare your own photos** of your waffles, cafe, and ambience
2. **Replace the files** in the `images/` folder with your own photos
3. **Keep the same filenames** (or update the paths in `index.html`)
4. **Recommended sizes:**
   - `hero.jpg`: 1920x1080px or larger (landscape)
   - `about.jpg`: 800x600px or larger
   - Product images: 600x600px (square works best)
   - Gallery images: 400x400px (square)

## Tips
- Use high-quality, well-lit photos
- Keep file sizes reasonable (compress if needed)
- JPG format works best for photos
- Make sure images show your waffles and cafe in the best light!
